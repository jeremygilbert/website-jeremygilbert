About Me

As Associate Dean for Journalism and the Knight Professor of Digital Media Strategy, he leads Medill's journalism program. His research and teaching focus on understanding the needs and desires of media audiences, experimenting with new technologies, like artificial intelligence, and exploring revenue strategies for existing and emerging media. He explores the intersection of technology and media, employing a human-centered design process to examine how new tools and techniques will affect the creation, consumption and distribution of media. Jeremy also oversees Northwestern's Knight Lab.

Experience

Before rejoining Northwestern, he helped The Washington Post identifying, creating, and executing unique digital products, storytelling experiences and digital strategies. Before that, worked for National Geographic develop and deploy its digital strategy and was an associate professor teaching media product design, interactive storytelling, web and print design tools and techniques for Medill and the Segal Design Institute at Northwestern University. Previously Jeremy led The Poynter Institute in rethinking and redesigning its industry leading website and served as an art director at a couple of newspapers.

  • Graphic Designer: Viant - Designed digital businesses for traditional retail vendors – focusing mainly on Web design for this internet consultancy.
