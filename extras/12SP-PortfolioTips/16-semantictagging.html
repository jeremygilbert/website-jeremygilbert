Always tag your content semantically

The best SEO strategy is to always tag semantically -- meaning you describe content as it is with your HTML. If it is the most important headline/header on the page it gets an H1, the second most important gets an H2. This is an area where best web design and production practices intersect with marketing. If you follow the rules you will be rewarded in the end. Do not try to trick Google. It never works for long.
