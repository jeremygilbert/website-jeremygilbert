I am the Director of Strategic Initiatives at The Washington Post. I help lead the newsroom to identify, create, and execute unique digital products and storytelling experiences. He works closely with The Post’s engineering, product design, graphics, audience, analytics and advertising teams.

In this role, I've reimagined election night experiences, created The Post's first virtual and augmented reality stories, told stories with photogrammetry, built a freelance network that changes how The Post covers national stories, architected The Post's machine generated storytelling system (Heliograf) and launched a new leadership vertical.
