FRANCE: 10 DAYS; 9 NIGHTS | 6.18.01


AN IMPOSING STRUCTURE NOT FAR FROM ISLE DE CITE, THE FRENCH LANGUAGE ACADEMY IS THE QUASI-FASCIST BODY DEDICATED TO THE PROTECTION OF THE ENLIGHTENED FRENCH LANGUAGE FROM SUCH CALLOW, IMPERIALIST OPPRESSORS LIKE: Le BIG MAC AND MICKEY MOUSE.
THE CAPITAL OF FRENCH LINGUAL PURITY

"A Swedish exchange student made me do it. When she took the cash and a ticket on another airline what red blooded American could resist. Unfortunately I proved equally unable to resist free Irish whiskey, beef fillets, mango slices, shrimp (boiled and fried) and several very unfortunate 'one last' glasses of wine..."

In the shadow of Notre Dame, the French Language Academy sits proudly in its Greco splendor. Inside French linguists debate the finer points of language that, though beautiful, only Frenchmen bother to speak.
