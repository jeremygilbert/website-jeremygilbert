A STOP-OVER FOR 12TH CENTURY CRUSADERS ON THE WAY TO THE HOLY LAND, THE RUINS OF THIS CHURCH IN SAINT ANDRE DES EAUX SERVED AS A HOLY PLACE, BURIAL GROUND AND EVEN A CAMPGROUND.
WAY POINT FOR THE PIOUS AND TOURISTS

"A weathered road sign points vaguely in the direction of the church ruins, in a town this small it's hard to believe it would be this hard to find anything, but the ruined church is nearly over-run by the expanding forest..."

Just south San Malo, somewhere in the heart of Norman farm country lies the small crossroads village of Saint Andre des Eaux. A collection of centuries old farmhouses cluster themselves around a pair of churches one ancient and one just a few hundred years old.
