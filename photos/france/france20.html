MANY OF THE SHAKESPEARE BOOK'S LESS FORTUNATE PATRONS HAVE NAPPED OR SLEPT IN THE UPSTAIRS LOFT. IN THE LOFT TRAVELERS PIN OR WRITE NOTES ON A MIRROR AND A SERIES OF BLACK AND WHITE PHOTOS COMMEMORATE FAMOUS VISITORS.
A BOOK STORE DEDICATED TO THE BARD

"On our way to supper we stopped at the Shakespeare bookstore. My parents had been there during the 1960s. Every inch of the walls and some of the floors are covered in books. There is a very steep, narrow staircase leading to a 'hidden' room..."

Jacqueline Kennedy shopped at Shakespeare books as have a parade of intellectuals and neer-do-wells. The proprietor, a kindly old man, still sits outside his shop and reads in the early afternoon. The clerks are Americans or Englishmen over for the summer or living as ex-patriots.
