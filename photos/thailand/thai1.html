SOUTHEAST ASIA: HALF A WORLD AWAY | 11.14.03


THE CENTRAL TEMPLE OF THE ROYAL WATT WITH THE ROYAL PALACE IN THE BACKGROUND. THE ROYAL PALACE IS IN THE CENTER OF BANGKOK'S OLD CITY BUT IS NO LONGER ACTUALLY A HOME FOR THE KING.
LIFE AFTER JET LAG

"[Sarah and I] poked around a central square in front of the palace that had been used for executions, but now had kite-fighting or taxi parking..."

The Royal palace is giant walled-in-square spreading over several blocks. Entering a giant gate, the Watt and Palace dominate the skyline. Brightly colored, two-story-tall warriors - wearing elf shoes - guard each of the enterances.

The Watt (a Buddhist temple) is decorated in brilliant colors and the towers are 'held up' by monkey warriors. Inside the spires are the bones of kings.
