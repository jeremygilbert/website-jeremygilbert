The power of XML was combined with Cascading Style Sheets which allowed BRACE to manipulate the presentation layer.

By classing all of the attributes of each BRACE page, minor changes to the style sheets could create very different looking pages.

BRACE partners are allowed to supply their own logo and graphics, choose background colors and even change the font or size of the type. All of this is automated so that sales people with no design experience can implement these design decisions.
EACH OF BRASSRING'S PARTNERS SELECTS WHAT LOGO, COLORS (THREE IN ALL - ONE DARK, ONE LIGHT AND ONE ACCENT) AND WHAT FONT THEY WILL USE.
