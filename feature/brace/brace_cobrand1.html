CO-BRAND
Perhaps the most challenging and important phase of BRACE construction was determining the level of customization available to BRACE cobrands.

BrassRing has more than one hundred partners who use BrassRing products to power 'career' sections of their site. One of the goals of BRACE was to make customization and setup of these partner areas simple while retaining the unique appearance of each site.

The partner sites vary wildly in their appearances but the XML output of the BRACE database meant that the data could be easily presented in different ways.
