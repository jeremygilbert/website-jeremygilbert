NEW BEGINNINGS:

I am 1,232 miles away from Chicago in a town where 24 screen multiplexes count as cultural monuments and yet in many ways I am infinitely more happy than I was in third largest city in the country. I am finally back in journalism.

In my 4 months working at The News-Press I have designed pages commemorating Steve Spurrier's resignation and doubletruck's about picnicking.
