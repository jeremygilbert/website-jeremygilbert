Reflecting on change: Elegant, bold, somber, but not maudlin. Those were the words the managing editor and I chose to describe what would become The News-Press' Sept. 11th special section.

The time line was originally the most critical element of the section. Overtime it became clear that a time line didn't tell a local story, but still an important element. The red line help tie tother the color theme and made a columnar time line linear - and therefore much easier to navigate.
