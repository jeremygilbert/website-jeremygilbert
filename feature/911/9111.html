Reflecting on change: Elegant, bold, somber, but not maudlin. Those were the words the managing editor and I chose to describe what would become The News-Press' Sept. 11th special section.

The cover would be the most difficult part. Our section was concentrating on the effect of the tragedy on local residents, but 911 was a national event and no local imagery would do it justice. Since our readers had experienced this as a 'television' event I decided to show the past year the same way.
