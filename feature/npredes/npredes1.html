THE NEWS-PRESS REDESIGN 2004

What we did, and why:
We constantly conduct research into readers' interests and needs, and we watch for trends. It became clear that our readers wanted expanded local content and an updated look. With this redesign, we offer three things: more local, regional and state news every day; three new sections to serve special reader interests; and a contemporary new look.
