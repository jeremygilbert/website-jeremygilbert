THE NEWS-PRESS REDESIGN 2004

Typography:
About 80 percent of the material on a newspaper page is text. We've selected new type for our words, both stories and headlines.

Headlines:
The primary news headlines will be Miller Headline, a serif type that is authoritative and highly readable. Relay, a sans-serif type, is crisp and contemporary. It will be used most often for feature stories.
