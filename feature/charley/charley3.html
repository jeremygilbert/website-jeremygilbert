HURRICANE CHARLEY '04:
Friday, August 13

Countdown to landfall.

All morning the design staff raced the storm to finish the weekend sections. Soon the Hurricane was stalled over Sanibel Island and strong winds began to pound the building.

Several different storm plans already existed - they differed depending on the severity of the storm and flooding. I went over the different scenarios with the design staff and made tough choices about who to take in case we had to evacuate.

By late afternoon Charley - now a 145-mph Category 4 storm brushed by Fort Myers on its way to Punta Gorda. The building began to twist and groan under its steel shutters.
