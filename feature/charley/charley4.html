HURRICANE CHARLEY '04:
Friday, August 13

Countdown to landfall.
...continued.

The normally calm and collected building maintenance staff was running up and down the halls screaming about parts of the roof coming off. The power flickered on and off as the generators strained to keep pace with the power demands.

Working in the dark because of balky generators, the designers hunkered down to put out a pair of 16-page sections. Soon information was pouring in photographers and reporters out investigating the aftermath.

The photo editors were editing mountains of photographs many frightening in their intensity. The winds were so severe that few images could be taken while storm was overhead. Instead the staff had captured people's horror and suffering as they surveyed the damage.
