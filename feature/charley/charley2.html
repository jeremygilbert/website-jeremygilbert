HURRICANE CHARLEY '04:
Thursday, August 12

Getting started.

The next morning the NOAA issued a warning saying that Charley would make landfall somewhere on the west coast of Florida. Still forecaster predicted that at worst the storm would reach Category 2 and the weather outside remained sunny and clear.

But that afternoon as the sky grew more menacing and the storm continued to gather force I tried to prepare my staff, update some basic design plans and finish Friday's front page.

By evening the storm was hovering off the coast of Florida and 10- to 13-foot flood waters seemed likely. At 2 a.m. the paper was finally out. But by 6 a.m. I was up again filling and stacking sand bags before work.
