HURRICANE CHARLEY '04:
Wednesday, August 11

Before the storm.

The newsroom was buzzing with news - but not anything about a storm in the Gulf of Mexico. The local congressman, Porter Goss, was nominated to head the CIA. It seemed highly unlikely that any bigger news would involve sleepy Southwest Florida.

Busy chasing the Goss story, almost no one was paying attention to a small tropical storm named Charley quietly spinning toward Florida. Over the years, Southwest Florida has had a number of near-miss hurricanes, but it had been 44 years since a hurricane had made landfall in the area.

No one was worried yet.
