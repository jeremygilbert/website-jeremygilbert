HURRICANE CHARLEY '04:
Saturday, August 14

Discovering the damage.
...continued.

The second day's newspaper featured a 32-page hurricane section. We now had a more complete picture of the devastation. Since most people in our coverage area lacked electricity we ran even more photos than the day before, including a doubletruck aerial of a Charlotte mobile home park where whole homes were gone. To try and capture the visual intensity of the storm we ran a front-page photo that filled the news box. It showed a dry boat storage shed crushed with dozens of boats scattered and smashed in the wreckage like children's toys.

Many of the carries had failed to show up or couldn't deliver their regular routes. Armed with a stack of papers, Jessica and I made sure that Sunday morning at least our neighbors could have a newspaper.
