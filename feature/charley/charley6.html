HURRICANE CHARLEY '04:
Friday, August 13

Countdown to landfall.
...continued.

I worked with the designers and photo editors to emphasize photoplay and to ensure the storm damage had a human face to it. The front page, that night and for the next two weeks, would follow a basic theme: strong and layered headlines, dominant photos, an overview of the storm-related events and a simple index to all the coverage inside.

As the staff trickled out late that night the nervous anticipation was palpable - what if that person's home was gone? Already I knew my neighborhood had been closed to all traffic - the damage and flooding were too severe.
