HURRICANE CHARLEY '04:
Saturday, August 14

Discovering the damage.

For a couple of hours I had slept on the floor of the newsroom, but I needed to find out what had happened to my home. Jessica and I stumbled through the newsroom and heard some early reports: Charlotte, the county to the north, had been hit hard - in places it was nearly destroyed.

Heading home, the landscape seemed alien. Trees were gone and buildings wrecked. The streets of our neighborhood were nearly impassable. Amazingly our house had survived intact.
